DAVID DE GRUIJL

Founder. Engineer. Healthcare. Biology. AI.

I’m the founder of Anto Biosciences, named after “The Father of Microbiology”, Antonie van Leeuwenhoek, who was the first person in human history to observe the microbial world. I’m from the Netherlands and studied in Delft, the city where he built his microscopes.

I care about individual differences in our biology: why some people, for example, respond well to life-saving drugs, while others don’t. A lot of that gap, I think, runs through the gut: digestion, absorption, and how the body meets medicine.
